# Order Fulfillment & Delivery Stories

## Story 5.1: Order Confirmation and Processing
**As a** customer  
**I want to** receive immediate order confirmation  
**So that** I know my purchase was successful and what happens next  

### Acceptance Criteria:
- [ ] Instant order confirmation email and SMS
- [ ] Order number and reference details
- [ ] Selected product and payment plan summary
- [ ] Next steps and expected timeline
- [ ] Customer service contact information

### Priority: High
