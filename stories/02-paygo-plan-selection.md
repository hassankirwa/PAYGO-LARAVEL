# PayGo Plan Selection Stories

## Story 2.1: View PayGo Options
**As a** customer  
**I want to** see all available PayGo payment plans for a product  
**So that** I can choose a payment schedule that fits my budget  

### Acceptance Criteria:
- [ ] Display weekly, monthly, and quarterly payment options
- [ ] Show duration options (6, 12, 18, 24 months)
- [ ] Display down payment requirements for each plan
- [ ] Show total cost including interest
- [ ] Compare savings vs. full upfront payment

### Priority: High
### Status: 📋 Pending

---

## Story 2.2: Calculate Payment Plan
**As a** customer  
**I want to** use an interactive calculator to customize my payment plan  
**So that** I can find the most suitable payment schedule  

### Acceptance Criteria:
- [ ] Adjust payment frequency (weekly/monthly)
- [ ] Modify down payment amount
- [ ] Change plan duration
- [ ] Real-time calculation of installments
- [ ] Display total interest and savings breakdown

### Priority: High
### Status: 🔄 In Progress

---

## Story 2.3: Compare Payment Plans
**As a** customer  
**I want to** compare different payment plans side by side  
**So that** I can understand the financial implications of each option  

### Acceptance Criteria:
- [ ] Side-by-side comparison of up to 3 plans
- [ ] Highlight differences in total cost, duration, installments
- [ ] Show early payment benefits
- [ ] Display late payment penalties
- [ ] Calculate cost-effectiveness for each plan

### Priority: Medium
### Status: 📋 Pending

---

## Story 2.4: Understand Terms and Conditions
**As a** customer  
**I want to** clearly understand the PayGo terms and conditions  
**So that** I know my rights and obligations  

### Acceptance Criteria:
- [ ] Display clear terms and conditions
- [ ] Explain payment obligations and consequences
- [ ] Detail IoT control and suspension policies
- [ ] Show customer rights and support options
- [ ] Provide FAQ section for common questions

### Priority: High
### Status: 📋 Pending

---

## Story 2.5: Select Preferred Plan
**As a** customer  
**I want to** select my preferred PayGo plan  
**So that** I can proceed to purchase with my chosen payment schedule  

### Acceptance Criteria:
- [ ] One-click plan selection
- [ ] Review selected plan details
- [ ] Modify plan before proceeding
- [ ] Save plan for later purchase
- [ ] Proceed to registration/checkout
