# Customer Product Discovery Stories

## Story 1.1: Browse Product Catalog
**As a** potential customer  
**I want to** browse the KOYO freezer and refrigerator catalog  
**So that** I can see all available products and their specifications  

### Acceptance Criteria:
- [ ] Display product categories (Freezers, Fridges, Accessories)
- [ ] Show product images, specifications, and pricing
- [ ] Filter products by category, price range, size
- [ ] Search functionality for specific models
- [ ] Responsive design for mobile and desktop

### Priority: High
### Status: 🔄 In Progress

---

## Story 1.2: View Product Details
**As a** potential customer

**So that** I can make an informed decision before purchasing  

### Acceptance Criteria:
- [ ] Display comprehensive product specifications
- [ ] Show high-quality product images (multiple angles)
- [ ] Display energy consumption and efficiency ratings
- [ ] Show warranty information

## Story 1.3: Compare Products
**As a** potential customer  
**I want to** compare different KOYO appliances side by side  
**So that** I can choose the best option for my needs  

### Acceptance Criteria:
- [ ] Select up to 3 products for comparison
- [ ] Display specifications in comparison table
- [ ] Highlight key differences
- [ ] Compare PayGo plan options for each product
- [ ] Save comparisons for later reference

### Priority: Medium
### Status: 📋 Pending

## Story 1.4: Check Product Availability
**As a** potential customer

### Acceptance Criteria:
